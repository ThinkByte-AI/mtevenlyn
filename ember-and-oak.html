Our Story

Your local, under new
management

Mt Evelyn Pizza & Pasta is your neighbourhood spot on York Road for fresh wood-style pizza, hearty pasta and a relaxed lounge bar. Under new management, we've reopened our doors — and the kitchen is now firing for lunch as well as dinner.

From the classics — Margherita, Hawaiian, Meat Lovers — to gourmet hand-finished pizzas like Lamb & Rocket and Truffle Mushroom, every pizza is made to order. Add a bowl of pasta, a side of cheesy garlic bread and a drink from the bar, and dinner's sorted.

Whether you're dining in, grabbing takeaway or having it delivered, the goal is simple: Great Food, Great Times — every time.
